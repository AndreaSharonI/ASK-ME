The water cycle is the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the Earth's surface. The Sun heats water in oceans, lakes, and rivers, causing some of it to evaporate into water vapour. As the vapour rises, it cools and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds. When the droplets become heavy, water falls back to Earth as precipitation, such as rain. Water then collects in rivers, lakes, and oceans, and the cycle begins again. This natural process helps distribute fresh water and supports plants, animals, and people.
